# Fleet Operations Policy

Department: Operations
Category: policy
Classification: internal
Version: 2026.1
Effective date: 2026-01-01
Owner: Head of Fleet Operations

## 1. Scope

Applies to all Acme Logistics owned and contracted vehicles operating from the Pune, Chennai and Delhi hubs.

## 2. Pre-trip inspection

Drivers complete a pre-trip check covering tyres, brakes, lights, load restraint, telematics device and documentation. Defects that affect safety prevent dispatch until cleared by the workshop.

## 3. Hours of service

- Maximum 9 driving hours per day, extendable to 10 hours twice per week.
- Minimum 30-minute break after 4.5 hours of driving.
- Minimum 11 hours of rest between shifts.

## 4. Route and delivery exceptions

Delays beyond 60 minutes are reported to the control tower, which informs the customer. Failed deliveries are re-attempted the next working day and recorded with a reason code.

## 5. Fuel and tolls

Fuel is purchased on the company fuel card only. Toll receipts are captured in the driver app. Cash purchases above ₹2,000 require hub-manager approval.

## 6. Incident handling

Road incidents are reported immediately to the control tower, followed by a written record within the shift including vehicle ID, location, time, description and any third-party details.
