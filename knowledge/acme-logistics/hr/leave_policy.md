# Leave Policy

Department: Human Resources
Category: policy
Classification: internal
Version: 2026.1
Effective date: 2026-01-01
Owner: HR Lead

## 1. Annual leave

Acme Logistics employees receive **18 days of paid annual leave** per calendar year. Hub supervisors and above receive 22 days. Leave accrues monthly.

## 2. Applying

Requests are submitted in the workforce app at least seven days in advance. Hub managers approve based on route coverage. During peak season (October to December), no more than 15% of a hub's drivers may be on leave at the same time.

## 3. Sick leave

Ten days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required after two consecutive days. Drivers must not operate a vehicle while taking medication that affects alertness.

## 4. Carry-forward

Up to five days may be carried into the next year and must be used by 28 February.

## 5. Unpaid leave

Unpaid leave beyond ten days requires Head of Operations approval.
