# Non-Conformance Procedure

Department: Quality
Category: procedure
Classification: internal
Version: 2026.1
Effective date: 2026-02-01
Owner: Quality Manager

## 1. Definition

A non-conformance is any product, process or document that does not meet a specified requirement, including undocumented changes and skipped SOP steps.

## 2. Immediate containment

1. Stop the affected process.
2. Quarantine suspect material with a red tag and move it to the hold area.
3. Identify the suspect window: from the last known good check to the detection point.
4. Raise a non-conformance report (NCR) within the shift.

## 3. Disposition options

| Disposition | Who decides |
| --- | --- |
| Use as is | Quality Manager (customer approval if specification affected) |
| Rework to specification | Quality lead with documented rework instruction |
| Repair | Quality Manager + Engineering |
| Scrap | Quality lead; Finance informed above ₹50,000 value |
| Return to supplier | Quality + Procurement |

## 4. Root cause and corrective action

Every NCR with repeat occurrence or customer impact requires an 8D-style analysis: containment, root cause, corrective action, verification of effectiveness and standardisation.

## 5. Escalation

Customer-detected defects and safety-related defects are escalated to the Quality Manager and Plant Director within one hour and follow the Production Escalation Procedure.

## 6. Records and metrics

NCR records are retained for seven years. Quality reports monthly on defects per million opportunities, cost of poor quality, and NCR closure timeliness.
