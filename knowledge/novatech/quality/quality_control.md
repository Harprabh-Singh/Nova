# Quality Control Procedure

Department: Quality
Category: procedure
Classification: internal
Version: 2026.1
Effective date: 2026-02-01
Owner: Quality Manager

## 1. Quality system

NovaTech operates a documented quality management system aligned to ISO 9001 and IATF-style process discipline. Every controlled characteristic has a defined measurement method, frequency and reaction plan.

## 2. Inspection stages

| Stage | What is checked | Frequency |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Incoming | Supplier material certificates, dimensional sample | Every lot |
| First article | Full dimensional and visual check after setup or change | Every setup |
| In-process | Key characteristics via control charts | Hourly or per defined count |
| Final | Function test, visual, packaging | Per shipping lot |

## 3. Statistical process control

Control charts are maintained for key characteristics. Reaction rules: one point outside control limits, or seven consecutive points on one side of the mean, requires the operator to stop and call Quality.

## 4. Measurement system

Gauges are calibrated on a defined interval and identified with a calibration label. Out-of-calibration gauges are removed from use immediately, and all product measured since the last valid calibration is quarantined.

## 5. Product release

Product is released only when inspection records are complete and no open non-conformance applies to the lot. Concessions require Quality Manager approval and customer agreement where the deviation affects specification.

## 6. Audits

- Layered process audits weekly by supervisors.
- Internal system audits quarterly.
- Supplier audits annually for critical suppliers.

Audit findings are tracked with owners and closure dates.
