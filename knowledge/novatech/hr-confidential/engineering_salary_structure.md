# Engineering Salary Structure

## 1. Purpose

Defines the compensation bands for engineering roles at NovaTech Manufacturing. This document is confidential and restricted to Human Resources leadership and enterprise administrators. It must not be shared with line managers or employees.

## 2. Engineering bands (annual fixed pay, INR)

| Band | Role | Minimum | Midpoint | Maximum |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| E1 | Graduate Engineer | 620,000 | 720,000 | 820,000 |
| E2 | Engineer | 850,000 | 1,010,000 | 1,180,000 |
| E3 | Senior Engineer | 1,250,000 | 1,480,000 | 1,720,000 |
| E4 | Lead Engineer | 1,800,000 | 2,120,000 | 2,450,000 |
| E5 | Engineering Manager | 2,500,000 | 2,950,000 | 3,400,000 |
| E6 | Head of Engineering | 3,600,000 | 4,300,000 | 5,000,000 |

## 3. Variable pay

Bands E1-E3 carry a 10% annual performance bonus target; E4 15%; E5 20%; E6 25%. Payout ranges from 0% to 150% of target based on plant and individual performance.

## 4. Progression rules

- Movement within a band is capped at 12% per cycle.
- Band promotion requires an open position, a calibrated performance rating and HR Manager plus Finance Manager approval.
- Off-cycle adjustments require the same approvals and a written business case.

## 5. Confidentiality

Access to this document is logged. Disclosure outside the approved audience is a disciplinary matter under the Employee Handbook and the Information Security Policy.
