# Employee Handbook

Department: Human Resources
Category: handbook
Classification: internal
Version: 2026.1
Effective date: 2026-01-01
Owner: Head of Human Resources

## 1. Purpose and scope

This handbook applies to all permanent employees, fixed-term employees, apprentices and contract staff working at any NovaTech Manufacturing site. Where a local site rule differs from this handbook, the stricter safety requirement always applies.

## 2. Working hours and shifts

- Plant operations run three shifts: A (06:00-14:00), B (14:00-22:00) and C (22:00-06:00).
- Corporate and engineering staff work a 40-hour week with core hours 10:00-16:00.
- Shift swaps require written approval from the shift supervisor at least 24 hours in advance.
- Overtime beyond 12 hours in a 24-hour period is not permitted for machine operators.

## 3. Attendance and time recording

Employees must record attendance through the plant access system. Missed punches must be corrected within three working days with supervisor confirmation. Three unexcused absences in a rolling quarter trigger a documented conversation with the line manager and HR.

## 4. Code of conduct

- Follow every published standard operating procedure without improvisation.
- Report unsafe conditions immediately, before attempting any correction.
- Do not share internal documents outside the company without written approval.
- Harassment, discrimination and retaliation are grounds for termination.

## 5. Dress code and personal protective equipment

Safety shoes, hearing protection and safety glasses are mandatory in all production areas. Loose jewellery and open sleeves are prohibited near rotating equipment. PPE is issued at no cost and replaced on wear.

## 6. Performance and development

Performance reviews run twice a year (March and September). Every employee has a development plan with at least two skill objectives. Certification for machine operation must be renewed every 24 months.

## 7. Grievance process

1. Raise the issue with your line manager.
2. If unresolved within five working days, escalate to the HR business partner.
3. If still unresolved, the Head of Human Resources convenes a review panel within ten working days.

Anonymous reports can be filed through the ethics line; NovaTech prohibits retaliation against anyone who reports in good faith.

## 8. Exit process

Notice periods are 30 days for employees and 60 days for managers. All company assets, access badges and documents must be returned on the last working day. Exit interviews are conducted by HR and summarised for the department head.
