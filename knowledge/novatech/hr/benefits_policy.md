# Benefits Policy

Department: Human Resources
Category: policy
Classification: internal
Version: 2026.1
Effective date: 2026-01-01
Owner: Head of Human Resources

## 1. Health insurance

- Group medical cover of ₹500,000 per year for the employee, spouse and up to two children.
- Parents can be added at employee cost during the annual enrolment window in November.
- Pre-existing conditions are covered from day one under the group scheme.
- Claims are cashless at network hospitals; reimbursement claims must be filed within 30 days.

## 2. Accident and life cover

All employees are covered for accidental death and permanent disability at three times annual fixed pay. Production staff receive an additional shift-risk rider.

## 3. Retirement and savings

- Provident fund contribution of 12% of basic pay, matched by the company.
- Gratuity as per statute after five years of continuous service.
- Optional voluntary provident fund contributions can be set in the HR portal.

## 4. Shift and site allowances

| Allowance | Amount | Eligibility |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Night shift premium | ₹450 per shift | Shift C operators and supervisors |
| Public holiday shift | 1.5x day rate | All shift employees |
| Site travel allowance | ₹12 per km | Inter-site travel on company duty |
| Tool allowance | ₹6,000 per year | Maintenance technicians |

## 5. Learning benefit

Up to ₹40,000 per employee per year for role-relevant certification, approved by the line manager and recorded by HR. Reimbursement requires a passing certificate within 12 months.

## 6. Wellbeing

- Confidential counselling through the employee assistance programme.
- Annual health check for all employees; biannual for Shift C.
- Ergonomics assessment on request for desk-based roles.

## 7. Claim process

Submit benefit claims in the HR portal with supporting documents. HR verifies within five working days; Finance disburses with the next payroll cycle. Expense-type reimbursements follow the Expense Policy approval thresholds.
