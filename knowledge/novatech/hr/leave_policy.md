# Leave Policy

Department: Human Resources
Category: policy
Classification: internal
Version: 2026.1
Effective date: 2026-01-01
Owner: Head of Human Resources

## 1. Annual leave entitlement

- Permanent employees receive **21 days of paid annual leave** per calendar year.
- Employees with more than five years of service receive **24 days**.
- Managers and above receive **26 days**.
- Leave accrues monthly at 1/12 of the annual entitlement and is available from the first day of employment on a pro-rata basis.

## 2. Carry-forward and encashment

Up to **7 unused annual leave days** may be carried into the next calendar year and must be used before 31 March. Days beyond that lapse. Encashment is allowed only at exit, capped at 15 days.

## 3. Applying for annual leave

1. Submit the request in the HR portal at least **10 working days** in advance for leave longer than two days.
2. Line manager approval is required; production staff also need shift-coverage confirmation from the shift supervisor.
3. Requests during a planned production ramp or quarter-end close may be deferred with a documented reason.
4. Approved leave appears on the shift roster within 24 hours.

## 4. Sick leave

- 12 days of paid sick leave per year.
- Absences longer than two consecutive days require a medical certificate.
- Work-related injury absence is handled under the Workplace Safety Policy and is not deducted from sick leave.

## 5. Casual and emergency leave

Six days of casual leave per year, maximum two consecutive days. Emergency leave can be approved retroactively by the line manager within 48 hours.

## 6. Parental and caregiver leave

- Primary caregiver: 26 weeks paid.
- Secondary caregiver: 4 weeks paid, to be used within six months of birth or adoption.
- Caregiver leave for a dependent's serious illness: up to 10 days per year.

## 7. Unpaid leave and sabbatical

Unpaid leave beyond 15 days requires department-head approval and HR endorsement. Sabbaticals of up to three months may be granted after five years of service.

## 8. Public holidays

The company publishes 12 public holidays each January. Employees working a public holiday shift receive compensatory time off at 1.5x or shift premium pay, per the Benefits Policy.
