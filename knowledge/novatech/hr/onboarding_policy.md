# Onboarding Policy

Department: Human Resources
Category: policy
Classification: internal
Version: 2026.1
Effective date: 2026-01-01
Owner: HR Business Partner, Talent

## 1. Pre-boarding (before day one)

- HR issues the offer pack, background-check consent and site-access request.
- IT & Security provisions accounts based on the role template in the Access Control Policy.
- The hiring manager nominates a buddy and prepares a 30-60-90 day plan.

## 2. Day one

1. Site safety induction (mandatory, two hours) covering emergency exits, assembly points and PPE.
2. Badge issuance and access-level confirmation.
3. Handbook, Leave Policy and Information Security Policy acknowledgement.
4. Equipment handover with asset registration.

## 3. First week

- Department orientation with the line manager.
- Quality and non-conformance awareness session for production and engineering hires.
- Read-and-sign for the SOPs relevant to the role.
- Introduction to NOVA, the internal knowledge assistant, and how to find policies.

## 4. Machine operator certification path

New operators may not run production equipment unsupervised until they complete:

1. Machine Operation SOP training and written assessment (minimum score 80%).
2. Ten supervised production hours on the specific asset class.
3. Sign-off by the shift supervisor and the Quality lead.

Certification is recorded in the training register and expires after 24 months.

## 5. 30-60-90 day reviews

| Milestone | Owner | Output |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Day 30 | Line manager | Role clarity check, training gaps |
| Day 60 | Line manager + buddy | Independent task ownership |
| Day 90 | Line manager + HR | Confirmation of probation |

## 6. Probation

Probation is 90 days for employees and 180 days for managers. Extension requires HR approval and a documented improvement plan.
