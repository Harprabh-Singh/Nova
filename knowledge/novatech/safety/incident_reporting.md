# Incident Reporting Procedure

Department: Safety
Category: procedure
Classification: internal
Version: 2026.2
Effective date: 2026-03-01
Owner: Safety Manager

## 1. What must be reported

Every workplace incident: injury, near miss, property damage, chemical release, fire, unsafe condition, and any machine failure that created a risk to people.

## 2. How to report a workplace incident

1. Make the area safe and get medical help if needed.
2. Tell your shift supervisor immediately - verbally is enough as the first step.
3. File the incident record the same shift with these fields: what happened, where, when, who was involved, machine or asset ID, severity, immediate actions taken and the reporter's name.
4. Preserve the scene and any damaged part for investigation unless it is unsafe to do so.
5. The supervisor acknowledges the record and assigns an investigator.

Incidents can be filed through the safety register, through the supervisor, or through the NOVA assistant, which collects the same required fields and creates the record.

## 3. Timelines

| Severity | Notification | Investigation report |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Critical (lost-time injury, fire) | Immediate to Safety Manager and Plant Director | 48 hours preliminary, 10 days final |
| High | Within 1 hour to Safety officer | 5 working days |
| Medium | Same shift | 10 working days |
| Low / near miss | Same shift | Reviewed in weekly safety meeting |

## 4. Investigation

Investigations use a structured root-cause method (5-why or fishbone) and must identify systemic causes, not only operator error. Corrective actions have named owners and due dates.

## 5. Confidentiality and non-retaliation

Medical details are confidential to occupational health. Reporting in good faith never leads to discipline; failing to report does.

## 6. Regulatory reporting

The Safety Manager decides on statutory notification. Reportable injuries are notified to the authority within the legally required window and logged in the statutory register.
