# Workplace Safety Policy

Department: Safety
Category: policy
Classification: internal
Version: 2026.1
Effective date: 2026-01-10
Owner: Safety Manager

## 1. Safety commitment

No production target justifies an unsafe act. Any employee may stop a job they believe is unsafe, without permission and without fear of retaliation.

## 2. Personal protective equipment

| Area | Mandatory PPE |
| --- | --- |
| Machining halls | Safety shoes, glasses, hearing protection |
| Press lines | Above plus cut-resistant gloves |
| Chemical store | Above plus apron and face shield |
| Visitor walkways | Safety shoes and high-visibility vest |

## 3. Lockout / tagout

Energy sources must be isolated, locked and tagged before any maintenance inside a machine envelope. Only the person who applied a lock may remove it. Group lockout uses a lock box controlled by the maintenance lead.

## 4. Hazard reporting

Report hazards and near misses the same day through the safety register or by telling the shift supervisor. Near-miss reporting is mandatory and is never used for discipline.

## 5. Permits to work

Hot work, confined-space entry, work at height above two metres, and electrical work above 415 V require a signed permit issued by the Safety officer and valid for one shift only.

## 6. Training

- Safety induction on day one for every joiner and contractor.
- Annual refresher for all plant staff.
- Quarterly emergency drills per the Emergency Response Plan.

## 7. Contractors

Contractors work under the same rules and must present competency evidence before badge issuance. The sponsoring department head is accountable for contractor compliance.

## 8. Health surveillance

Noise-exposed roles receive annual audiometry. Chemical-exposed roles receive biannual health checks. Results are confidential to occupational health.
