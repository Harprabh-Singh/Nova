# Emergency Response Plan

Department: Safety
Category: plan
Classification: internal
Version: 2026.1
Effective date: 2026-01-10
Owner: Safety Manager

## 1. Emergency types covered

Fire, chemical spill, medical emergency, machine entrapment, power failure, gas leak, natural disaster and security threat.

## 2. Universal first actions

1. Raise the alarm using the nearest call point.
2. Protect yourself and others; do not attempt rescue beyond your training.
3. Call the emergency control room on extension 999 and state location, type and casualties.
4. Evacuate to the assembly point for your zone unless told to shelter in place.

| Zone | Assembly point |

Shift supervisors perform a headcount using the shift roster and report to the incident controller.

## 4. Roles during an emergency

- **Incident controller** (senior manager on site): overall decisions, liaison with authorities.
- **Safety officer**: technical advice, permits, containment.
- **Fire wardens**: sweep and confirm evacuation of their zone.
- **First aiders**: casualty care until medical services arrive.
- **Maintenance**: utility isolation.

## 5. Medical emergencies

First aid rooms are located in Plant 1 and Plant 2. Serious injuries are transported to the partner hospital; HR informs the family and records the event under the Incident Reporting Procedure.

## 6. Chemical spill

Small spills (under 5 litres, known substance) are contained by trained staff using the spill kit. Larger or unknown spills require evacuation of the area and Safety officer response.

## 7. Drills and review

Full evacuation drills run quarterly; results are reviewed by the safety committee within ten days, and corrective actions are tracked to closure.
