# Preventive Maintenance Plan

Department: Manufacturing Operations
Category: plan
Classification: internal
Version: 2026.1
Effective date: 2026-02-01
Owner: Maintenance Lead

## 1. Maintenance tiers

| Tier | Frequency | Performed by | Typical tasks |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Daily autonomous | Every shift | Operator | Clean, inspect, check levels, report anomalies |
| Weekly | Weekly | Maintenance technician | Lubrication, filter check, belt tension |
| Monthly | Monthly | Maintenance technician | Alignment, calibration check, electrical inspection |
| Annual overhaul | Yearly | Maintenance + vendor | Spindle service, drive replacement, safety re-certification |

## 2. Scheduling

Preventive maintenance is scheduled during planned downtime windows agreed with production planning at least two weeks ahead. Deferral beyond seven days requires Manufacturing Operations head approval.

## 3. Critical spares

Spares for critical assets (M-200 series presses, M-300 CNC cells) are held at a minimum stock level defined per asset. Consumption below the reorder point triggers an automatic purchase requisition under the Procurement Policy.

## 4. Condition monitoring

Vibration and temperature sensors on critical spindles report to the maintenance dashboard. Two consecutive readings above the warning threshold create a maintenance task; readings above the alarm threshold stop the machine and follow the Machine Failure SOP.

## 5. Records

Every maintenance action records date, asset, technician, tasks performed, parts used and machine state on handover. Records are retained for seven years for audit and warranty purposes.
