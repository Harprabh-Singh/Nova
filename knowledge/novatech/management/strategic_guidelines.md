# Strategic Guidelines 2026

Department: Management
Category: guideline
Classification: confidential
Version: 2026.1
Effective date: 2026-01-01
Owner: Plant Director

## 1. Strategic priorities

1. **Reliability** - lift overall equipment effectiveness from 71% to 78% by year end.
2. **Quality** - reduce cost of poor quality to under 1.2% of revenue.
3. **Safety** - zero lost-time injuries; near-miss reporting above 20 per month as a leading indicator.
4. **Digitisation** - make every policy and SOP retrievable through the internal knowledge assistant.
5. **Supply resilience** - dual-source the top 20 critical components.

## 2. Investment guardrails

- Capital investment is prioritised for bottleneck assets and safety compliance first.
- Payback expectation is under 30 months for capacity projects.
- Automation proposals must include a reskilling plan for affected operators.

## 3. Operating cadence

| Forum | Frequency | Focus |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Shift handover | Every shift | Output, quality, safety events |
| Plant leadership review | Weekly | Escalations, delivery risk |
| Capex committee | Monthly | Investment decisions |
| Business review | Quarterly | Strategy progress and reforecast |

## 4. Decision principles

- Safety outranks output, always.
- Prefer reversible decisions taken quickly over perfect decisions taken late.
- Escalate with a recommendation, not only a problem.
- Document the decision and its rationale so it can be audited later.

## 5. Confidentiality

This document is confidential and limited to management and department heads. Do not share externally or with teams outside the approved distribution.
