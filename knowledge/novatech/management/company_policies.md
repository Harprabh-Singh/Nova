# Company Policies Overview

Department: Management
Category: policy
Classification: internal
Version: 2026.1
Effective date: 2026-01-01
Owner: Plant Director

## 1. Purpose

This overview explains how NovaTech Manufacturing's policy framework fits together and who owns each area. It is the starting point when you are not sure which document answers your question.

## 2. Policy map

| Topic | Document | Owner |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Employment terms, conduct | Employee Handbook | Human Resources |
| Time off | Leave Policy | Human Resources |
| Pay-adjacent benefits | Benefits Policy | Human Resources |
| Reimbursements | Expense Policy | Finance |
| Buying goods and services | Procurement Policy, Approval Matrix | Procurement, Finance |
| Running machines | Machine Operation SOP | Manufacturing Operations |
| Machine breakdowns | Machine Failure SOP, Production Escalation | Manufacturing Operations |
| Safety and incidents | Workplace Safety, Emergency Response, Incident Reporting | Safety |
| Product quality | Quality Control, Non-Conformance | Quality |
| Systems and data | Information Security, Access Control, Security Incident Response | IT & Security |
| Engineering work | Engineering Standards, Deployment SOP, Change Management | Engineering |

## 3. Policy governance

- Each policy has a named owner and a review cycle of at most 12 months.
- Versions use a `YEAR.SEQUENCE` scheme, for example 2026.1 then 2026.2.
- Only the active version is authoritative; superseded versions are retained for audit.
- Material changes are communicated to affected employees before the effective date.

## 4. Exceptions

Policy exceptions require the policy owner's written approval, a defined expiry date, and a record in the exception register. Safety rules are not subject to exception.

## 5. Where to ask

Employees can ask the NOVA knowledge assistant, which answers only from documents they are authorised to read and always cites the source policy and version. If the assistant cannot verify an answer, contact the policy owner directly.
