# Information Security Policy

Department: IT & Security
Category: policy
Classification: internal
Version: 2026.1
Effective date: 2026-01-05
Owner: Head of IT & Security

## 1. Information classification

| Class | Meaning | Examples |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Public | Approved for external release | Brochures, published certifications |
| Internal | Default for company information | Policies, SOPs, process documents |
| Confidential | Limited to a defined group | Compensation data, supplier pricing, customer contracts |
| Restricted | Highest sensitivity, need-to-know | Credentials, security architecture, legal case files |

Documents must carry their classification. When in doubt, treat information as Internal and ask the owner.

## 2. Acceptable use

- Company systems are for business use; incidental personal use must not affect security or performance.
- Do not install unapproved software on plant or engineering systems.
- Do not connect personal storage devices to production equipment.
- Do not paste confidential content into external AI tools or public websites.

## 3. Credentials

Passwords must be at least 14 characters and unique to NovaTech. Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for remote access and administrative accounts. Credentials are never shared, never written in tickets, and never stored in documents.

## 4. Data handling

- Confidential data is encrypted in transit and at rest.
- Removable media must be encrypted and registered.
- Printing confidential documents requires badge-release printing and immediate collection.
- Retain data only as long as the retention schedule requires.

## 5. Email and phishing

Report suspected phishing using the report button; never forward it. IT & Security responds within one hour during business hours.

## 6. Third parties

Vendors receive access only after a security review, with least-privilege accounts that expire automatically at contract end.

## 7. Compliance

Violations are handled under the Employee Handbook. Deliberate exfiltration of company data is grounds for termination and legal action.
