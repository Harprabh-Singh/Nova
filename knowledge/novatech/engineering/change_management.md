# Change Management Policy

Department: Engineering
Category: policy
Classification: internal
Version: 2026.1
Effective date: 2026-01-15
Owner: Engineering Manager

## 1. Change categories

| Category | Definition | Approval |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Standard | Pre-approved, low risk, documented procedure | Shift supervisor |
| Normal | Affects equipment, product or process | Engineering Manager + Quality lead |
| Major | Affects safety systems, product specification or customer commitments | Engineering Manager + Quality Manager + Manufacturing Operations head |
| Emergency | Needed to restore production or remove a hazard | Verbal by department head, documented within 24 hours |

## 2. Change record contents

Every change record includes: description, reason, affected assets, risk assessment, validation plan, rollback plan, training impact and document updates.

## 3. Change advisory review

Normal and major changes are reviewed weekly by the change advisory group (Engineering, Quality, Manufacturing Operations, Safety). The group may request additional validation or a pilot run.

## 4. Freeze periods

No normal or major changes are deployed during quarter-end production pushes or during customer audits, unless approved as emergency changes.

## 5. Post-implementation review

Major changes receive a review within 14 days covering realised benefit, defects introduced, and whether documentation and training were completed.

## 6. Non-compliance

Undocumented changes are logged as non-conformances under the Non-Conformance Procedure and may require re-validation of affected production.
