# Expense Policy

Department: Finance
Category: policy
Classification: internal
Version: 2026.1
Effective date: 2026-01-01
Owner: Finance Controller

## 1. Principles

Expenses must be business-necessary, reasonable, documented and submitted in the correct period. NovaTech reimburses actual costs only; per-diem substitution is not allowed unless stated below.

## 2. Submission and deadlines

- Submit claims within **30 days** of the expense date.
- Claims older than 60 days require Finance Controller approval.
- Original invoices are mandatory above ₹1,000; card statements alone are not sufficient.

## 3. Reimbursement limits

| Category | Limit | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Domestic hotel (metro) | ₹6,500 per night | Manager and above ₹8,500 |
| Domestic hotel (non-metro) | ₹4,000 per night | |
| Meals while travelling | ₹1,200 per day | Alcohol is not reimbursable |
| Local transport | Actuals | Ride-hailing receipts required |
| Air travel | Economy class | Business class only above 6 hours with department-head approval |
| Client entertainment | ₹3,000 per guest | Names and business purpose required |
| Mobile and internet | ₹1,500 per month | Roles with field duty |

## 4. Expense approval thresholds

| Claim total | Approver |
| --- | --- |
| Up to ₹10,000 | Line manager |
| ₹10,001 - ₹50,000 | Department head |
| ₹50,001 - ₹200,000 | Finance Manager |
| Above ₹200,000 | Finance Controller and CFO |

Approval of purchases (as opposed to reimbursement of expenses) is governed by the Procurement Policy and the Approval Matrix.

## 5. Non-reimbursable items

Personal entertainment, traffic fines, lounge memberships, personal device purchases, and any expense already covered by a company allowance.

## 6. Advances

Travel advances up to ₹25,000 may be requested five working days before travel and must be settled within 15 days of return.

## 7. Audit

Finance samples 10% of claims each month. Deliberate misstatement is treated as misconduct under the Employee Handbook.
