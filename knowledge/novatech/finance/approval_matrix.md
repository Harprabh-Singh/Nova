# Approval Matrix

Department: Finance
Category: matrix
Classification: internal
Version: 2026.2
Effective date: 2026-04-01
Owner: Finance Controller

## 1. How to read this matrix

The matrix defines who may commit company funds or approve exceptions. Amounts are in Indian Rupees (₹) and refer to the total order value including taxes. Where two approvers are listed, both are required.

## 2. Capital purchases (equipment, tooling, plant assets)

| Value band | Approver | Secondary control |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Up to ₹25,000 | Line manager | Procurement spot check |
| ₹25,001 - ₹100,000 | Department head (Engineering Manager, Manufacturing Operations head, or equivalent) | Two quotations attached |
| ₹100,001 - ₹500,000 | Finance Manager + department head | Capex form and payback note |
| ₹500,001 - ₹2,500,000 | Finance Controller + CFO | Capex committee review |
| Above ₹2,500,000 | Managing Director | Board note |

Example: a ₹80,000 equipment purchase is approved by the relevant **department head**. An Engineering Manager can approve engineering equipment in this band; the request still needs two quotations and a purchase order before any commitment.

## 3. Operating expenditure

| Value band | Approver |
| --- | --- |
| Up to ₹10,000 | Line manager |
| ₹10,001 - ₹50,000 | Department head |
| ₹50,001 - ₹200,000 | Finance Manager |
| Above ₹200,000 | Finance Controller + CFO |

## 4. People decisions

| Decision | Approver |
| --- | --- |
| Hiring within approved headcount | Department head + HR Manager |
| New headcount | CFO + HR Manager |
| Salary revision outside the annual cycle | HR Manager + Finance Manager |
| Overtime above 20 hours per month per employee | Department head |

## 5. Operational exceptions

| Decision | Approver |
| --- | --- |
| Deviation from a published SOP | Department head + Quality lead |
| Release of non-conforming material | Quality Manager (mandatory) |
| Emergency spares up to ₹50,000 | Shift supervisor with department-head confirmation |
| Production line stoppage beyond four hours | Manufacturing Operations head |

## 6. Delegation

Approvers may delegate authority in writing for a maximum of 30 days. Delegation cannot exceed the delegate's own band by more than one level and must be recorded with Finance.
