# Expense Policy

Department: Finance
Category: policy
Classification: internal
Version: 2026.2
Effective date: 2026-04-01
Owner: Finance Controller

## 1. What changed in 2026.2

- Submission deadline shortened from 30 days to **21 days** from the expense date.
- Domestic metro hotel limit increased to **₹7,500** per night.
- Meal limit while travelling increased to **₹1,500** per day.
- A new mileage rate of **₹12 per km** replaces the previous flat travel allowance for personal-vehicle use.
- Digital receipts are now accepted for all values; paper originals are no longer required.

## 2. Principles

Expenses must be business-necessary, reasonable, documented and submitted in the correct period. NovaTech reimburses actual costs only.

## 3. Submission and deadlines

- Submit claims within **21 days** of the expense date.
- Claims older than 45 days require Finance Controller approval.
- Digital invoices are acceptable; the claim must show vendor, date, amount and tax details.

## 4. Reimbursement limits

| Category | Limit | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Domestic hotel (metro) | ₹7,500 per night | Manager and above ₹9,500 |
| Domestic hotel (non-metro) | ₹4,500 per night | |
| Meals while travelling | ₹1,500 per day | Alcohol is not reimbursable |
| Personal vehicle | ₹12 per km | Log start and end readings |
| Air travel | Economy class | Business class only above 6 hours with department-head approval |
| Client entertainment | ₹3,000 per guest | Names and business purpose required |
| Mobile and internet | ₹1,800 per month | Roles with field duty |

## 5. Expense approval thresholds

| Claim total | Approver |
| --- | --- |
| Up to ₹10,000 | Line manager |
| ₹10,001 - ₹50,000 | Department head |
| ₹50,001 - ₹200,000 | Finance Manager |
| Above ₹200,000 | Finance Controller and CFO |

Purchases of goods, equipment or services are governed by the Procurement Policy and the Approval Matrix, not by this policy.

## 6. Non-reimbursable items

Personal entertainment, traffic fines, lounge memberships, personal device purchases, and any expense already covered by a company allowance.

## 7. Advances and audit

Travel advances up to ₹30,000 may be requested five working days before travel and must be settled within 15 days of return. Finance samples 10% of claims monthly.
