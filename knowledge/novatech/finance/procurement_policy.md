# Procurement Policy

Department: Procurement
Category: policy
Classification: internal
Version: 2026.2
Effective date: 2026-04-01
Owner: Head of Procurement

## 1. Scope

This policy covers all purchases of goods, equipment, services, spare parts and software for NovaTech Manufacturing. Capital purchases are additionally governed by the Approval Matrix.

## 2. Purchase approval thresholds

| Purchase value | Required approval | Quotations |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Up to ₹25,000 | Line manager | 1 quotation |
| ₹25,001 - ₹100,000 | **Department head** | 2 quotations |
| ₹100,001 - ₹500,000 | Finance Manager and department head | 3 quotations |
| ₹500,001 - ₹2,500,000 | Finance Controller and CFO | 3 quotations + tender note |
| Above ₹2,500,000 | Managing Director with board note | Formal tender |

An equipment purchase of **₹80,000** therefore falls in the ₹25,001 - ₹100,000 band and requires **department-head approval with two comparative quotations**. Department heads include the Engineering Manager for engineering assets and the Manufacturing Operations head for plant assets.

## 3. Purchase requisition flow

1. Requester raises a purchase requisition with specification and business justification.
2. Procurement validates vendor status and existing rate contracts.
3. Approval is obtained per the threshold table above.
4. Procurement issues the purchase order; no work may start before a PO exists.
5. Goods receipt and quality inspection are recorded before invoice release.

## 4. Emergency purchases

For production-stopping breakdowns, the shift supervisor may authorise spares up to ₹50,000 with verbal approval from the department head, followed by a written requisition within 24 hours. Emergency purchases are reported monthly to Finance.

## 5. Vendor management

- New vendors require registration, tax verification and a quality questionnaire.
- Critical-component vendors are audited annually by Quality and Procurement.
- Single-source purchases above ₹100,000 require a documented justification.

## 6. Conflict of interest

Employees must declare any relationship with a vendor. Related-party purchases require Finance Controller approval regardless of value.

## 7. Segregation of duties

The person who raises a requisition may not approve it, and the approver may not perform goods receipt for the same order.
